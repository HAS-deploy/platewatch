PlateAware — Support

PlateAware is a public-roadway infrastructure transparency map: it shows where fixed ALPR, red-light, traffic, toll, and school-zone cameras already exist on public roads, using the DeFlock community map (built on OpenStreetMap) and other open public datasets.

Common questions

Where does PlateAware's camera data come from?
The pins on the map come from the DeFlock community dataset at maps.deflock.org, which is itself built on OpenStreetMap and distributed under the ODbL open data license. We ingest the dataset on a weekly schedule on our server, then deliver delta updates to your device. A thin US-wide seed dataset ships inside the app so the map works on first launch even before the first sync completes.
Does PlateAware detect police officers or warn me about active enforcement?
No. PlateAware shows only fixed, permanent infrastructure that is visible from public roads and already documented in open public datasets. It does not detect, identify, locate, or alert on any individual person, including law-enforcement officers. There is no live "police nearby" feature, no officer reporting, and no real-time enforcement detection.
Why is the map mostly empty when I pan to a rural area?
The underlying open dataset has more coverage in dense urban areas than in rural ones — that reflects real community contributions to OpenStreetMap and DeFlock, not a bug. If you know of a fixed camera that should be on the map, you can submit a report from the Settings tab. Reports enter a moderation queue and are not visible to other users until verified.
How does Camera Ahead work, and why does it need background location?
Camera Ahead is a premium feature that fires a local notification before you reach a known fixed camera. To do that, iOS needs the "Always" location permission. We request it only the moment you turn the feature on — never at launch. You can turn Camera Ahead off any time in Settings, which immediately stops background location use.
What's the difference between free and Pro?
Free includes the map, your current location, nearby camera pins, basic camera details, and up to 5 searches per day. PlateAware Pro adds Camera Ahead proximity alerts, pre-drive route scan, offline state packs, advanced filters, density score, saved routes, faster sync, and full report history.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → PlateAware → Cancel. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current period.
How do I restore a previous purchase?
On the paywall screen tap "Restore Purchases." As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID that made the original purchase, it will restore automatically.
How do I delete my data?
Open PlateAware → Settings. If you have not signed in with Apple, tap Delete All Data — this wipes the install-trial stamp, onboarding state, and the local entitlement on this device. If you have signed in with Apple, tap Delete Account — this clears all PlateAware data on the device and opens Apple's Sign in with Apple manager so you can revoke the grant. No email round-trip is required either way.
