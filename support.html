PlateAware — Support

PlateAware is a map of fixed, publicly documented roadway cameras (ALPR, red-light, traffic, toll, speed, and school-zone cameras) using the DeFlock community map, built on OpenStreetMap.

Common questions

Where does the camera data come from?
From the DeFlock community map, which is built on OpenStreetMap data (ODbL). The app ships with a starter dataset and loads cameras around you from DeFlock's public server when you share your location. If that server can't be reached, the app keeps showing the cached data.
Does PlateAware detect police or active enforcement?
No. PlateAware shows only fixed, permanent infrastructure already documented in open public data. It has no live police, officer, or enforcement alerts.
Why is the map sparse in some areas?
Coverage reflects what the OpenStreetMap / DeFlock community has mapped, which is denser in cities than in rural areas.
How does Camera Ahead work?
Camera Ahead (Pro) shows a local notification when you approach a known camera. Turning it on in Settings asks for "Always" location and notification permission, which iOS needs to alert you while the app is in the background. Turn it off in Settings at any time.
What's included in Pro?
Free includes the camera map, camera details, and directions showing cameras along the fastest route. Pro adds Camera Ahead alerts, the privacy-preferring route comparison with a camera density score, and saved routes. New installs get 7 days of Pro free.
How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PlateAware → Cancel, or tap Manage Subscription in PlateAware. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current period.
How do I restore a purchase?
On the PlateAware Pro screen tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.
How do I delete my data?
PlateAware has no account. Settings → Delete All Data deletes your saved routes, Camera Ahead settings, and preferences on the device. Uninstalling removes everything.
