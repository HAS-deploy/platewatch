Privacy Policy — PlateAware

Last updated: 2026-09-25

This policy describes what data PlateAware ("the app") handles. PlateAware has no user accounts and does not operate its own server. Questions: support@medbillresolve.com.

Data stored only on your device

  • A cache of public camera locations (from a dataset bundled with the app and from the DeFlock public map, see below).
  • Routes you choose to save, your Camera Ahead setting, onboarding state, and other app preferences.
  • Your location. PlateAware uses your location on your device to center the map, show nearby cameras, and fire Camera Ahead alerts. The app does not upload your location to us, and we do not store it.

Anonymous usage analytics (PostHog)

To understand how the app is used, PlateAware sends product-interaction events to PostHog (us.i.posthog.com), for example: screens viewed, onboarding steps, whether location permission was granted, route-scan mode used, paywall views, and subscription purchase or restore results (product ID, price, and App Store transaction ID). Each event includes the app version, build, device locale, subscription status, and days since install, and is associated with a random identifier generated for this install (after a first purchase, a hashed per-app device identifier). Events do not include your name, email, location coordinates, addresses you enter, or saved routes. As with any internet request, PostHog receives your device's IP address when events are sent. Analytics are not used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Anonymous analytics. When it is off, no analytics events are sent.

Services the app contacts

  • DeFlock public map (overpass.deflock.org). To load cameras near you, the app requests camera data for a rectangular map area around your current location. The request contains the map area only, with no account or identifier. DeFlock's data comes from OpenStreetMap (ODbL).
  • Apple Maps. Addresses you type for directions are sent to Apple to find places and calculate routes, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store (StoreKit). Handles subscription purchases and restores. We never see your payment details.
  • PostHog. Anonymous usage analytics as described above (PostHog privacy).

PlateAware does not use advertising networks, ad identifiers (IDFA), App Tracking Transparency, or data brokers, and does not sell data.

Permissions

  • Location — While Using the App. Centers the map near you and loads nearby cameras. If you decline, the map opens at a default region and you can pan manually.
  • Location — Always and Notifications. Requested only if you turn on the Pro Camera Ahead alerts in Settings. iOS monitors areas around nearby known cameras and PlateAware shows a local notification when you approach one. Turn it off any time in Settings.

PlateAware does not access your camera, photos, contacts, or microphone.

Your controls

  • Settings → Delete All Data deletes your saved routes, Camera Ahead settings, and app preferences on this device.
  • Revoke permissions in iOS Settings → PlateAware; uninstalling removes all app data from the device.
  • To ask about analytics data associated with your install, email support@medbillresolve.com.

Children

PlateAware is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change; the date at the top reflects the latest change.
